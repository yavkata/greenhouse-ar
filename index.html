Placing it on the plot

  1. Mark the four corners first: 3.76 m east–west, 4.50 m north–south.
  2. Stand a few metres south of the spot, facing north, and tap View on your plot.
  3. Sweep the phone slowly over the ground until it finds the surface, then tap to place.
  4. The glass roof faces you when it lands. Drag it onto the stakes and twist with two fingers until it lines up. Size stays locked at 1:1.

Requirements

AR needs a phone with ARCore (Android, in Chrome) or ARKit (iPhone, in Safari), and the page must be opened over https.
